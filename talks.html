Double Categories

Definition 1.1 (Double category) A double category \(\mathbb{C}\) consists of the following data: - a category \(\mathbb{C}_0\), whose objects we call 0-cells, and whose arrows we call tight 1- cells \(f: X \rightarrow X'\); - a category \(\mathbb{C}_1\), whose objects we call loose 1-cells \(M: X \not\rightarrow Y\), and whose arrows we call 2-cells

\[ a_{M,N,P}: (M \circ N) \circ P \Rightarrow M \circ (N \circ P) \]
