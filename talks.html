Double Categories

Definition 1.1 (Double category) A double category \(\mathbb{C}\) consists of the following data:

  1. a category \(\mathbb{C}_0\), whose objects we call 0-cells, and whose arrows we call tight 1- cells \(f: V \rightarrow V'\);

  2. a category \(\mathbb{C}_1\), whose objects we call loose 1-cells \(N: V \not\rightarrow W\), and whose arrows we call 2-cells

  1. two functors \(\text{src}, \text{tgt} : \mathbb{C}_1 \rightarrow \mathbb{C}_0\) called source and target respectively;

  2. composition and identity functors \(\text{comp} : \mathbb{C}_1 \times_{\mathbb{C}_0} \mathbb{C}_1\) and \(\text{id} : \mathbb{C}_0 \rightarrow \mathbb{C}_1\);

  3. natural families of globular isomorphisms in \(\mathbb{C}_1\):

These data are required to satisfy the following coherence axioms which manifest as equations between composite 2-cells.
